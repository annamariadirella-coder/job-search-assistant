# Final review checklist

## Vacancy

- Original employer posting verified, or clearly labeled unverified.
- Same role checked against the private tracker.
- Location, languages, employment terms and known compensation considered.
- Mandatory criteria distinguished from preferences.

## Evidence

- Every material experience claim has a source.
- No unconfirmed tool proficiency, ownership, metrics or achievements added.
- Supervision, contribution and direct implementation distinguished.
- Decisive uncertainties answered or disclosed.
- Direct applicant corrections incorporated with their intended scope.

## CV

- One coherent professional identity for the role.
- Relevant scope and outcomes visible early.
- Accurate role titles, dates and metrics.
- Natural wording and appropriate job terminology.
- Readable, selectable text and predictable document structure.
- Final rendered file checked for layout, spelling and completeness.

## Delivery

- Documents delivered for the applicant's final review.
- No application or outreach sent without authorization.
- Private records and screenshots kept out of the public project.
- Research and verification limitations disclosed.
