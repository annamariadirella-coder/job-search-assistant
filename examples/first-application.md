# A first application — fictional walkthrough

Every person, employer, job description and metric on this page is fictional. This example teaches the workflow; it is not an available vacancy or a tested interview prediction.

## Starting CV excerpt

**Marta Rossi — Customer Operations Manager**

Northwind Services, 2020–2026. Managed a team of eight, led a support-process redesign, worked with Engineering on an automated ticket-routing project and reported service KPIs. Earlier CV wording says: “Built automation that improved service efficiency.”

Search constraints: remote from Spain; English and Spanish; Customer Operations leadership. Compensation to be clarified. No existing application to the fictional employer.

## Fictional job

**Customer Operations Lead — CedarCloud**

Remote from Spain. The role requires team leadership, cross-functional process improvement and KPI reporting. It partners with Engineering on routing and support-tool changes. Personally coding integrations is not required. Prior B2B SaaS experience is preferred.

## Two decisive questions and fictional answers

**What did you personally do on the automation project?**

“I mapped the process, defined routing requirements, supervised testing and trained the team. Engineering coded it.”

**Is the efficiency result measured?**

“Median resolution time fell from eight to five days over six months. I can confirm that baseline and timeframe.”

## Reviewable Evidence Bank update

- Confirm: owned the operational workflow, requirements, testing coordination and team rollout.
- Confirm: median resolution time fell from eight to five days over six months.
- Narrow: replace “built automation” with the specific operational contribution.
- Do not claim: personally coded the routing integration.
