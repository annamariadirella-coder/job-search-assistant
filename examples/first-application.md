# A first application — fictional walkthrough

Every person, employer, job description and metric on this page is fictional. This example teaches the workflow; it is not an available vacancy or a tested interview prediction.

## Try it yourself

Open [Start here](../START-HERE.md). Paste [the starting CV](demo-inputs/cv.txt) and its starter message into a private assistant conversation. Then provide [the fictional JDs](demo-inputs/job-description.md) and [sample tracker](demo-inputs/tracker.csv). Answer using [the fictional confirmations](demo-inputs/confirmations.md). Compare the output with [the evidence records](demo-inputs/evidence-bank.md) and [CV excerpt](demo-inputs/tailored-cv.md).

The correct outcome is a draft for CedarCloud, exclusion of the already-applied HarborCloud role, and an explicit warning that neither fictional employer can be verified. Salary and hiring eligibility remain unresolved. Do not submit anything. See [the walkthrough review](../FLOW-REVIEW.md) for the checks and their limits.

Northwind Services, 2020–2026. Managed a team of eight, led a support-process redesign, worked with Engineering on an automated ticket-routing project and reported service KPIs. Earlier CV wording says: “Built automation that improved service efficiency.”

Search constraints: remote from Spain; English and Spanish; Customer Operations leadership. Compensation starts unknown. The sample tracker includes HarborCloud, but not CedarCloud; absence from this sample does not establish a real applicant's complete history.

## Three questions and fictional answers

**Confirm the team/KPI scope and the automation contribution.**

“I managed the eight-person service team and reported service KPIs. I mapped the process, defined routing requirements, supervised testing and trained the team. Engineering coded it.”

**What compensation or employment constraints affect targeting?**

“For this simulation, my minimum is €50,000 gross annual base and I want employment remotely from Spain. Unpublished pay is okay for research, but must be checked before submission.”

**Is the efficiency result measured?**

“Median resolution time fell from eight to five days over six months. I can confirm that baseline and timeframe.”

## Final gate

Check the real employer's availability, compensation and eligibility to employ from Spain. Review the CV. The applicant submits it and records the application in the tracker. The assistant does not submit it.

In this fictional run the gate stays closed: employer availability and employment conditions are unverified, and the starting CV is an excerpt. The prepared text is useful for review, but cannot be described as ready to submit. Preserve the confirmed records privately to continue in a new session.
